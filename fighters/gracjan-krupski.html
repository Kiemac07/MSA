Fighter profile

I’m a 19-year-old Muay Thai and boxing fighter representing MSA Leeds. I’ve been training since the age of 12 and fighting since I was 15. What started as a hobby and recreational training quickly became a passion after I fell in love with fighting from my first time stepping into the ring.

I’ve fought across England on a variety of shows, which has led to opportunities to compete internationally in Italy and Thailand for Team GB, where I won gold.

My fighting style is aggressive, relentless and entertaining. I’m always looking to push the pace, put pressure on my opponents and make every fight exciting.

Looking ahead, my goal is to continue developing as a fighter, build on my experience and progress into the professional ranks soon.
