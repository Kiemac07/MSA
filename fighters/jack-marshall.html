Fighter profile

I’m a 17-year-old Muay Thai fighter with roughly two years of consistent training. My passion for the sport started long before I stepped into a gym, and it has now become an obsession.

Since starting, I’ve had 2 senior fights and 2 junior fights, with a 2-0-0 senior record and a 1-1-0 junior record. I also became Junior Novice SANDEE Champion in only my second fight.

I’m a pressure fighter who likes to move forward, refusing to be put on the back foot and constantly looking to cause damage.

Looking ahead, my goal is to become a more fluid and well-rounded fighter, continuing to sharpen my craft, progress through the ranks and eventually compete in professional bouts.
